# Heat advice on the last-resort card

When no plan keeps the safe room within its limits until the next recharge, the last-resort plan (#160) shows a fixed list next to the honest window. The texts live in `@cold-reserve/i18n` (`lastResort.*`). The actions the plan may list are `LAST_RESORT_ACTIONS` in `@cold-reserve/contracts`. Each line comes from public-health guidance, checked on 2026-10-02. The card gives general tips, never medical protection, and says so.

| Line | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Close the curtains on the sunny side. | UKHSA, [Beat the heat](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/beat-the-heat-hot-weather-advice/beat-the-heat-staying-safe-in-hot-weather): close windows and curtains in rooms that face the sun. WHO, [Heatwaves: how to stay cool](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/heatwaves-how-to-stay-cool): cover windows to block sunlight. |
| Drink water often, even without thirst. | UKHSA, Beat the heat: drink fluids regularly through the day. WHO: drink water regularly. |
| Use a fan only while the room is below 35 °C. | WHO: above 35 °C a fan may not prevent heat-related illness. [Lancet Planetary Health 2024](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanplh/article/PIIS2542-5196(24)00030-5/fulltext) advises against fans above 35 °C. This is also the guard rule already in place (#107). |
| Signs of heat stroke: confusion or lack of coordination; hot skin that is not sweating; fast heartbeat or fast breathing; fainting, or a seizure. | UKHSA, Beat the heat (heatstroke symptoms). WHO: faint, stop sweating, delirium, losing consciousness. |
| If you are worried, call 112 (911 in Houston). | WHO: call the emergency services first. The number follows the place, not the language (#366): 112 is the European emergency number (Turin and the reference home), 911 the US one (Houston); UKHSA says 999, the UK number. |

Left out on purpose: actions with no source in this table, such as a wet cloth on the skin (#160).
